# Marsad v0.1.3

Selecting a card now draws its exposure as edges to "anything", so openness is
something you see rather than only read.

## Added

**Exposure as edges, on the selected card.** Openness has been two rows of text
on the card because drawing it for every unprotected workload is the hairball
that made the graph unreadable — two lines per workload converging on one point,
carrying nothing the danger ring did not already say. On a cluster where 129 of
156 workloads are unprotected that is 258 lines. None of that changes, and the
overview is untouched.

It does not apply to the one card somebody just clicked. A row of text states
the fact but does not show the shape, and the shape is what a graph is for. Two
dashed lines on the selected card cost nothing and answer the question directly.

They are dashed rather than solid because no rule declares this. It is what
happens in the absence of one, and it must not read as a written allow. Sources
go left and destinations right, the same axis the layered layout puts every
other edge on; where a card sits too near the edge of the stage for the side
that matches the flow, the pill moves to the other side and the arrowhead
carries the direction — a card reading "open to anything" while drawing nothing
looks broken.

## Upgrading

```sh
helm upgrade marsad oci://ghcr.io/fathiq/charts/marsad --namespace marsad
```

Nothing to change in your values.
