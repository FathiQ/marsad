# Marsad v0.1.2

One fix on top of 0.1.1. It landed after 0.1.1 was tagged, so 0.1.1 does not
carry it.

## Fixed

**A small namespace was magnified until it filled the screen.** Sigma scales
whatever it is given until it fills the viewport, so selecting a namespace with
three workloads blew those three up until they did. Card *drawing* is clamped to
a readable band but card *positions* are not, so the cards stayed the size they
always are while the gaps between them grew to most of the screen: three cards
adrift in an empty column, reading as broken rather than as small.

One layout unit is one pixel at 100%, so refusing to magnify past 1:1 is the
whole fix. A graph larger than the viewport is untouched — it still shrinks to
fit — and a graph smaller than it is now drawn at its own size and centred. The
camera's maximum ratio goes up with it: framing three workloads naturally needs
about 4.2 and the ceiling was 4, which was clamping exactly the cases this is
meant to fix.

Found while chasing a report of the graph zooming out on a real cluster. That
report turned out to be a pre-0.1.1 build, but this was underneath it.

## Upgrading

```sh
helm upgrade marsad oci://ghcr.io/fathiq/charts/marsad --namespace marsad
```

Nothing to change in your values.
