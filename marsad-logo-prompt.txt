Design a logo for Marsad — an open-source developer tool.

WHAT IT DOES
Marsad reads the network policies declared in a Kubernetes cluster and draws them
as an interactive map: which workloads can reach which, on which ports, and which
are protected by nothing at all. It is strictly read-only — it observes and never
changes anything. Its users are platform and security engineers, who run it
beside a terminal.

THE NAME
Marsad (مرصد) is Arabic for "observatory" — the place you watch from. The tagline
is "the observatory for your Kubernetes network policies". The mark should carry
that idea: patient observation, a wide field of view, something watched rather
than touched.

VISUAL DIRECTION
A geometric, minimal mark built from simple primitives — arcs, circles, straight
lines — with the precision of an engineering diagram rather than the softness of
a consumer brand. Think of the visual family of Grafana, Cilium, Linear, Vercel:
confident, restrained, unmistakable at small size.

Suggested directions, any one of which is enough on its own:
  - an observatory dome with its shutter open, the opening reading as a slit of sky
  - an aperture or iris formed of a few overlapping blades
  - a node-and-edge fragment — two or three connected points implying a larger
    network beyond the frame
  - a radar or concentric sweep, suggesting a field being watched

COLOUR
Primarily monochrome, with one accent. The product palette is a deep blue-black
background (#0a0d13) and a single blue accent (#5aa9ff), with green (#3ddc84)
reserved for "allowed" and red (#ff6b6b) for "exposed". Use at most two colours
in the mark. It must survive as a flat single-colour glyph.

DELIVERABLES
A square logomark on a transparent background, plus a horizontal lockup with the
wordmark "Marsad" set in a clean geometric sans (Inter, Söhne or similar) at a
comfortable optical weight beside the mark. Show it on both a dark and a light
background.

HARD CONSTRAINTS
  - Must remain legible at 16x16 px as a favicon and terminal glyph.
  - Must work as a flat, single-colour silhouette — no gradients, bevels, drop
    shadows or 3D.
  - No text inside the mark itself. The wordmark sits beside it.
  - No telescopes pointed at stars, no eyeballs, no magnifying glasses, no
    shields, no padlocks, no ships' helms, no globes wrapped in circuit traces,
    no hexagon-with-a-K Kubernetes derivative, no generic "AI" glow.
  - Avoid anything that implies blocking or enforcement — Marsad never changes a
    cluster, and a padlock or shield would misrepresent the product.
  - Avoid decorative Arabic calligraphy pastiche; a subtle structural nod to
    Islamic geometric pattern is welcome, an ornamental one is not.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHORTER VARIANT (for models that do better with less)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A minimal geometric logo for "Marsad", an open-source Kubernetes network
observability tool. The name means "observatory" in Arabic. An observatory dome
with an open shutter, reduced to a few clean arcs and lines — engineering
diagram, not illustration. Flat, two colours at most: near-black and a single
blue accent (#5aa9ff). Must read at 16px and as a single-colour silhouette.
Square mark on transparent background. No text, no shields, no padlocks, no eyes,
no telescopes, no gradients.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
JUDGING THE RESULTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The mark is a good one if it:
  1. Still reads at favicon size with the detail thrown away.
  2. Survives being filled in one flat colour.
  3. Says "observe", not "defend". Marsad has no write path and the mark should
     not imply one.
  4. Does not need the wordmark to be recognisable after a week of use.
  5. Sits comfortably beside the Kubernetes, Prometheus and Cilium marks without
     imitating any of them.
